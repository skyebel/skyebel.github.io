hello!

I’m an undergraduate in the Departments of Cognitive Science and MathematicsUC San Diego ☀️, double majoring in Cognitive Science (Machine Learning & Neural Computation) and Mathematics–Computer Science, with a minor in Chinese Studies. I work on the border between computational neuroscience and neuro-AI — comparing internal represenations of biological and artificial networks and building EEG pipelines to uncover neural dynamics.

Right now I’m a research assistant in the Neural Crossroads Laboratory under Dr. Rangel, and I postered at the California Neurotechnolongy Conference (CNTC) @UC Berkeley as software lead of a Triton NeuroTech low-cost sEMG prosthesis project. This past summer I attended the Neuromatch Academy NeuroAI program, where I compared RNN-GRU models of motor cortex against real macaque recordings — my favorite project so far.

Outside the lab, I sit on the board of The Catalyst, a UCSD Undergraduate Research Hub research journal. In the sphere of cognitive science and neuroscience, I am actively involved as Project Director for Triton NeuroTech @UCSD and Professional Relations Lead for the Cognitive Science Student Association (CSSA) @UCSD.
