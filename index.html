hello!!

I am an incoming second-year undergraduate student at UC San Diego, doubling majoring in Cognitive Science (Machine Learning & Neural Computation) and Mathematics–Computer Science with a minor in Chinese Studies. I work on the border between computational neuroscience and neuro-AI, from comparing internal represenations of biological and artificial networks to building EEG pipelines to uncover neural dynamics.

Right now I’m a research assistant in the Neural Crossroads Laboratory under Dr. Rangel. This recent summer I attended the Neuromatch Academy NeuroAI program, where I compared the representational geometry, dynamical trajectory, and behavior of MotorNet RNN-GRUs against real macaque motor cortex recordings: my favorite project so far! Wrapping up my first academic year, I postered at the California Neurotechnolongy Conference (CNTC) @UC Berkeley after successfully completing a Triton NeuroTech sEMG prosthesis project as Software Lead.

Outside the lab, I sit on the board of The Catalyst, a UCSD Undergraduate Research Hub paper journal. In the sphere of cognitive science and neuroscience, I currently aid as Project Director for Triton NeuroTech and as Professional Relations Lead for the Cognitive Science Student Association (CSSA).
